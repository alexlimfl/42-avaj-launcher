- **`@startuml` and `@enduml`**: Marks the beginning and end of the UML diagram.
- **`class`**: Defines a class.
- **Access Modifiers**:

    - `-` for private
    - `#` for protected
    - `~` for package-private
- **Relationships**:
    - `<|--` for inheritance (generalization)
    - `-*` for composition (a strong association)
    - `<|..` for realization (interface implementation)
    - `-o` for aggregation (a weak association)
